# Mekmaa POS — Cashier Guide

## 1. Sign in

1. Open https://fridge.mekmaa.com/.
2. Enter your assigned staff email and password.
3. If prompted to change a temporary password, complete that step first.
4. Open **Register session** before attempting checkout.

Staff accounts are created by a superadmin. Customers do not need accounts
or passwords.

## 2. Open your register

1. Navigate to **Register session**.
2. Review the assigned opening cash.
3. Enter the opening cash amount required by your allocation.
4. Select **Open register session**.

Each staff member has a separate register session. Another cashier's open
session does not authorize you to sell.

## 3. Make a cash sale

1. Open the POS page.
2. Select products and their quantities.
3. Review the cart and total.
4. Select the cash payment option.
5. Choose **Complete order**.
6. Review the confirmation details.
7. Confirm the sale and enter the cash received when prompted.
8. Check the calculated change and provide the receipt.

The checkout validates the reviewed total and active session.

## 4. Record a card sale

Use the card payment option during checkout.

**Important:** The application records the card payment but does not charge
a bank card. Complete the payment using the shop's separate payment terminal
and confirm it succeeded before recording the transaction as paid.

## 5. Sell on customer credit

1. Select an existing customer or create the customer's contact record.
2. Add products to the cart.
3. Choose **Buy now, pay later**.
4. Record any initial cash or card payment, or enter zero.
5. Review and confirm the transaction.

The unpaid balance is recorded against the customer.

Customer credit limits and any enabled Optimized Debts restrictions still apply.

## 6. Collect customer payments

1. Open **Customers** or the POS debtors section.
2. Select the customer and the unpaid sale or opening balance.
3. Enter the collection amount.
4. Choose the payment method.
5. Confirm the collection.
6. Provide the payment receipt where available.

Partial payments are supported.

Cash collections increase the current register's expected cash. Card
collections are recorded separately.

Do not record a collection twice if the connection fails. The application
includes recovery handling for interrupted requests.

## 7. Record petty cash

1. Open **Petty cash**.
2. Choose **Cash out** or **Cash in**.
3. Enter the amount.
4. Provide a category and explanatory note.
5. Select **Save entry**.

Cash-out entries reduce register cash; cash-in entries increase it.

## 8. Close your register

1. Navigate to **Register session**.
2. Review the displayed expected cash.
3. Physically count the money.
4. Enter **Counted closing cash**.
5. If there is a difference, provide the required reason.
6. Select **Close session**.

Never enter the expected amount without counting the actual cash.

The session history retains closing amounts and differences.

## 9. Common problems

**Checkout disabled:** Open your own register session.

**Insufficient stock:** Ask an authorized manager to review stock.

**Credit rejected:** Check customer credit restrictions and ask management
to review the account.

**Incorrect sale:** Contact an admin or superadmin. Use the authorized
refund or correction workflow; do not create an unrelated compensating sale.

**Unexpected cash difference:** Recount the cash, review collections and
petty cash, and record the correct closing amount with a reason.

## 10. End-of-shift checklist

- Confirm completed transactions and receipts.
- Review customer collections.
- Review petty-cash entries.
- Count physical cash.
- Record the actual closing balance.
- Close your own register session.
- Report unresolved discrepancies to management.
